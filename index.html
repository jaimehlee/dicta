How we verify

Dicta shows what company leaders actually said, so you can read the original instead of someone else's summary of it.

  1. We take the quote from the original source: an earnings release, filing, call transcript, interview or post.
  2. A person checks every word against that source before it goes up, and we link to it.
  3. We keep quotes short and mark trimmed text with "…". The link has the rest.
  4. We label the channel. Posts on an executive's personal account are marked as personal, because companies don't always treat those as official disclosure.
  5. We tag related companies, like suppliers, customers and competitors.
  6. We fix mistakes in the open. Every correction is listed on the Corrections page.

Not investment advice

Everyone sees the same content. Nothing here is tailored to your holdings, and nothing here tells you to buy or sell.
